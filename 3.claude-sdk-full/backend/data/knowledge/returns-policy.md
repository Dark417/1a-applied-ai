# Returns and refunds policy

Acme Outdoor accepts returns within 60 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of us receiving the item.

Used or worn gear can be returned within 30 days for store credit only. Safety equipment (climbing harnesses, helmets, ropes) cannot be returned once the packaging seal is broken.

## How to start a return

Log in, open the order, and click "Return item". You will get a prepaid label by email. Returns from outside the EU and US require the customer to pay shipping.

## Exchanges

We do not do direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order. Pro and enterprise tier customers get free return shipping worldwide.
