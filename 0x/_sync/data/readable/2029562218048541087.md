- **Nano Banana 2 can now handle interior design end to end**
  - Turns a sketched floor plan into a 4K 3D rendering with accurate dimensions
  - Takes photos of each room
  - 1-click furniture changes
  - Used to cost $100k and months; now cents and minutes
  - Its grasp of dimension and 3D space works like a personal designer in your pocket
- **Step-by-step tutorial on OpenArt**
  - **Step 1 · Render the floor plan**
    - Go to http://openart.ai → edit image → select Nano Banana 2 → upload floor plan → type prompt
  - **Step 2 · Realistic photo: living room**
    - Highlight the room you want to photograph and draw a camera to show the angle
    - Upload that as a reference image and use:
    ```text
    realistic interior render of a modern wooden cabin living room, viewed from the camera
    ```
  - **Step 3 · Realistic photo: kitchen**
    ```text
    Ultra-realistic interior render of a small modern wooden cabin kitchen, viewed from kitchen entry area, looking towards the sink side as indicated in the floor plan.

    The layout and furniture placement must match the floor plan
    ```
  - **Step 4 · Change furniture**
    - Upload both the living room photo and your sofa as reference images, prompt `change the sofa`
    - You can highlight a specific area for precise edits
  - **Step 5 · Make a video**
    - Use the living room and kitchen photos as start and end frames
