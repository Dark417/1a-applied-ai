- **Learn to build evals, folks!**
- You're **not really doing recursive self-improvement (RSI)** if you are not doing evals
  - Agents generating the evals for you? **They are pretty bad at it**
- Give agents a **concrete way to measure results** and stay on track with a long-term goal
  - It's easier to **hill climb** when things are clearly measured and well defined
- Probably **one of the most important skills** anyone in the space can have today
- No matter how many agents and how much intelligence you throw at a problem — **you won't go far without proper evals**
- The @dair_ai team has been spending a ton of time tinkering with and building evals
