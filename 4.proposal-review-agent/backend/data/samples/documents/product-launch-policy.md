# Product Launch Policy

Owner: Product Operations. Applies to every new product, major feature, and new-market launch.

## 1. Security review

Every new product or major feature must complete a security review before general availability (GA). The review is requested through the Security team's intake form at least four weeks before the planned GA date. All findings rated critical must be resolved before GA; high findings need a remediation plan with dates. Beta programs limited to fewer than 500 invited users may start before the review completes, but may not process production customer data.

## 2. Privacy review

A privacy review is required when a launch collects new categories of personal data, targets new user groups, or changes where data is stored. The privacy review confirms the lawful basis, retention period, and data location. Launches aimed at children require an additional children's privacy review; see the Data Privacy Standard.

## 3. New markets

Launching in a new country requires terms of service and a privacy notice in the official local language, reviewed by regional counsel. Regional counsel may approve an English-only launch for up to 90 days when a translation is in progress. Pricing pages must follow local rules; in the EU, prices shown to consumers include VAT.

## 4. AI features

Customer-facing features that generate text, images, or answers with AI should tell users that AI is involved, for example with a label near the generated content. Customer data may never be used to train third-party models without explicit, recorded consent from the customer.

## 5. Launch checklist

- Security review complete, criticals resolved
- Privacy review complete (if applicable)
- Localised legal documents for each new market
- Marketing claims reviewed against the Marketing Claims Guidelines
- Support team trained, rollback plan documented
