MARKETING CLAIMS GUIDELINES

Owner: Marketing, reviewed by Legal. Applies to web pages, ads, emails, social posts, and sales decks.

1. Email marketing
Marketing emails may only go to people who opted in. Every marketing email includes a working unsubscribe link and our postal address. Purchased or rented email lists are never used.

2. Superlatives
Words like "best", "#1", "fastest", or "leading" should be supported by independent third-party evidence, cited in the material or in a footnote. The VP of Marketing may approve clearly subjective puffery such as "the best coffee in the office".

3. Comparative claims
When we compare our product to a named competitor, the claim must be backed by documented, reproducible testing that we keep on file, including methodology, dates, and versions tested. Legal may approve a comparison based on published third-party benchmarks instead.

4. Discounts and promotions
Promotions state the end date and any conditions. Discounts above 40 percent off list price require written CFO approval before announcement.

5. Giveaways
Sweepstakes and giveaways should publish official rules including eligibility, prize value, and odds of winning.
