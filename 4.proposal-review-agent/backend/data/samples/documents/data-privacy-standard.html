Data Privacy Standard

Owner: Data Protection Officer. This standard applies to all processing of personal data by the company and its processors.

1. Scope

Personal data is any information relating to an identified or identifiable person, including names, email addresses, device identifiers, and location data.

2. Lawful basis

Before any processing starts, the team must record the lawful basis (consent, contract, legal obligation, or legitimate interest) in the data inventory. Processing without a documented lawful basis is prohibited.

3. Children

Services directed at children under 13 must obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting any personal information from a child, including an email address or a username that identifies the child. Acceptable methods include a signed consent form, a credit-card verification, or a video call with a parent. A checkbox saying "I am a parent" is not verifiable consent.

4. Data location and transfers

Personal data of EU residents is stored in our EU regions. It must not be stored or processed outside the EU or EEA unless Legal has approved a transfer mechanism, such as Standard Contractual Clauses with a transfer impact assessment.

5. Retention

Personal data should be deleted or anonymised no later than 24 months after the last interaction with the customer. The Data Protection Officer may approve a longer period when there is a documented business or legal justification.

6. Processors

Vendors that process personal data on our behalf need a data processing agreement and a current vendor risk assessment.
