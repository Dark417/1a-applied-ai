# Product care and warranty

All Acme Outdoor gear carries a 2 year warranty against manufacturing defects. Normal wear, UV damage, and damage from misuse are not covered. Warranty claims are handled through a support ticket with photos of the defect.

## Down sleeping bags

Store uncompressed in the large mesh sack. Wash only with a down-specific detergent in a front-loading machine, then tumble dry on low with dryer balls until fully dry. Never dry clean.

## Backpacks

Hand wash with mild soap and lukewarm water. Do not machine wash; it damages the foam and coatings. Air dry away from direct sun. Re-tension the hip belt buckles annually.

## Water filters

Backflush after every trip. Never let a used filter freeze; ice cracks the hollow fibres and the filter must be replaced. Replace the filter cartridge after roughly 1000 litres.
