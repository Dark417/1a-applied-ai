# Shipping

Orders ship from our warehouse in Rotterdam within 1 business day. Standard shipping is free on orders over 75 USD; otherwise it costs 6.90 USD.

## Delivery times

- EU: 2 to 4 business days.
- UK and Switzerland: 3 to 6 business days, customs handled by us (DDP).
- US and Canada: 5 to 8 business days.
- Rest of world: 7 to 14 business days.

Express shipping (1 to 2 business days in the EU) costs 14.90 USD and must be selected at checkout.

## Tracking

Every shipment gets a tracking link by email as soon as the carrier scans it. If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation.
